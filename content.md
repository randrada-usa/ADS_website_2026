## Digital Bayan: Empowering Communities Through Tech Literacy

### _id: "initiative-community"

photo: public/images/about.JPG

### Overview

Digital Bayan is our flagship community outreach initiative dedicated to bridging the digital divide. Initiated in October 2025, the project focuses on bringing essential digital skills directly to the grassroots level, ensuring that emerging technologies are accessible to those who need them most.

### Our Approach

We believe that technology should empower, not exclude. Digital Bayan tackles the implications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in digital and media literacy. We partner with local organizations and tech experts—including the Holotech Society, the Sangguniang Kabataan of Rumagayray, San Enrique, and Developers Connect Inc. (DevCon) Iloilo—to deliver impactful, hands-on learning experiences.

### Impact in Action

During our pilot activity on February 28, 2026, we successfully engaged students through interactive workshops. Our curriculum covers:

AI Fundamentals: Introducing what AI is, along with its real-world pros and cons.

Media & Information Literacy: Teaching students critical thinking skills to differentiate AI-generated media from reality, spot fake news, and practice effective fact-checking.

Hands-On Creation: Guided by our volunteers, students use tools like Loveable to "vibe code" and create their own games, transitioning from passive consumers to active digital creators.

Interactive Robotics: We incorporate engaging activities, such as navigating chalk mazes with AI-generated Mbots provided by Devcon Iloilo.

Through Digital Bayan, we are not just teaching technology; we are building a foundation of critical thinking and creative confidence for the next generation of digital citizens.

### Unveiling the Dark Web: Empowering Cyber-Resilient Youth

_id: "event-community"
photo: "public/images/techtalk.jpg"

### Overview

Recognizing that Filipino youth are highly active online yet increasingly vulnerable to cyber threats and misinformation, we launched "Unveiling the Dark Web and Its Cyber Mysteries". Held on March 6, 2025, at the CPMT Building, this initiative was designed to boost digital literacy and cybersecurity awareness among grassroots undergraduate students.

### Our Approach

Aligned with Google’s "Be Internet Awesome" initiative, the project featured immersive, expert-led sessions:

- Navigating the Shadows: Speakers Jasmar Oclares and Ian Troy Pahilga explored the differences between the Surface, Deep, and Dark Web, analyzing real-world threats and demonstrating how to check for compromised data using tools like "Have I Been Pwned?".
- The Ethical Hacker's Toolkit: Mr. Vinray Alumbro guided students through modern malware-free cybercrimes, social engineering tactics, and the methodology of penetration testing.

### Impact in Action

The alternative class successfully engaged 253 students from diverse academic backgrounds. By equipping them with practical ethical hacking concepts and data privacy frameworks, we empowered these learners to transition from vulnerable users into informed, responsible digital citizens capable of navigating the internet safely.

### Startup Spark: Unlocking Your Inner Steve Jobs

### _id: "event-workshop"

### photo: "public/images/pitch.jpg"

### Overview

On February 29, 2024, our organization—then operating as the Google Developer Student Clubs at the University of San Agustin—hosted "Startup Spark: Unlocking Your Inner Steve Jobs". Held at the 6th Floor Function Room of Hotel Sta. Monica, this engaging workshop was part of the university's U-Week Alternative Classes in partnership with DEVCON.

### The Core Philosophy

The event challenged students to embrace bold innovation under the guiding quote: "Take Risks. Huge Impact.". Built around the idea that "Every Product has it's Story," the session taught participants that pitching is fundamentally about distilling the core essence of a project into a compelling narrative that gets people excited.

### Program Highlights

- Unveiling the Narrative: The first major speaker session, "Exploring the Stories Behind Every Product," was led by Marie Ariessa Lane Ko, a Product Manager at StackTrek Enterprise. She shared industry expertise to help students enhance their skills in delivering compelling and powerful pitches.
- Live Pitching & Judging: Following an afternoon snack break and icebreaker, students put their new skills into practice during Speaker Session 2, which featured hands-on pitching activities culminating in a live judging segment.
